你好 (Hello)

20 тапсырма • Тыңдалым · Айтылым · Оқылым · Жазылым

Тапсырмалар ▾
🎧 Тыңдалым (5 тапсырма)
  • Listen to 5 greetings and identify if they are saying "nǐ hǎo" or "nǐn hǎo".
  • Listen to a short dialogue and write down the pinyin you hear.
  • Listen to a recording of "nǐ hǎo" and identify the correct tones (3rd + 3rd).
  • Identify which image matches the spoken greeting (e.g., meeting a teacher vs. a friend).
  • True/False: The speaker is greeting a single person (nǐ hǎo) vs multiple people (nǐmen hǎo).
🗣️ Айтылым (5 тапсырма)
  • Greet your partner in Chinese.
  • Roleplay: Greet your teacher respectfully using "您好" (nín hǎo).
  • Practice the tone change rule: Say "nǐ hǎo" changing the first 3rd tone to a 2nd tone.
  • Say hello to a group of imaginary classmates using "你们好" (nǐmen hǎo).
  • Read aloud fluently: "你好！你好！"
📖 Оқылым (5 тапсырма)
  • Match the Chinese characters "你" and "好" with their English meanings.
  • Identify the pinyin for the character "您".
  • Read a short chat bubble and determine the context (a greeting).
  • Select the correct character to complete the phrase: __ 好 (A: 你, B: 我).
  • Find the character for "good" among a list of similar-looking characters.
✍️ Жазылым (5 тапсырма)
  • Practice writing the strokes for the character "一" (one).
  • Practice writing the strokes for the character "八" (eight).
  • Trace the character "你" (you).
  • Trace the character "好" (good).
  • Write the pinyin for "Hello" with the correct tone marks.

📖 Оқылым (5 тапсырма)
  • Match "谢谢" and "再见" with their English translations.
  • Read a two-line dialogue and fill in the missing response: A: 对不起. B: _____.
  • Identify the pinyin for "不客气".
  • Match images of people interacting (waving goodbye, bowing) to correct phrases.
  • Identify the incorrect character in: "对不气" (should be 起).
✍️ Жазылым (5 тапсырма)
  • Practice writing the basic character "不" (no/not).
  • Trace the characters for "再见" (goodbye).
  • Write the pinyin for "You're welcome".
  • Write out the characters for "Thank you" using a stroke-order guide.
  • Translate and write in pinyin: "It doesn't matter / That's okay."
